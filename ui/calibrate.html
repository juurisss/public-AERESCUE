Camera calibration

Open console to select and start two camera previews first. Keep both cameras fixed during calibration.

Camera heading calibration

Use the same physical marker in both views. Enter that marker's known perpendicular depth for each pair, then repeat at different depths and positions. The marker's lateral position is calculated and does not need to be entered.

The world heading convention is +z straight out from the camera baseline. Heading offsets are measured from that direction and are fitted independently for Camera A and Camera B.

Enter a point depth, then click a feature in Camera A and the same feature in Camera B.

Wall HFOV calibration (legacy)

Use a flat wall parallel to the camera baseline. Cameras must be level, parallel and facing straight toward the wall, with Camera A on the left. Measure from the camera lenses, not the laptop edges.

Wall distance means straight ahead at 90° to the wall, not the diagonal distance to each clicked point. All points must lie on this same wall. This estimates HFOV only; it does not correct lens distortion, camera rotation or an off-center optical axis.

Click a distinctive wall point in Camera A, then the same physical point in Camera B. Repeat at five or more positions spread left to right.
